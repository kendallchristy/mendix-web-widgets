Outside labels get room on every side, so they no longer overlap the pie. Labels above and below get longer lines, names that don't fit are cut short, and on narrow charts the legend names the slices while the labels show the share. The hole size can now be set in pixels as well as in percent of the radius, and the total in the hole shrinks to fit it.
