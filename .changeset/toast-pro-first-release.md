First release of Toast Pro: toasts that show on load, from an expression or from a Boolean attribute, close after a milliseconds expression, run actions before and after showing and closing, and hold buttons or custom widgets. Includes a shared stack per corner, pause on hover, a countdown bar, loading toasts, group keys, a close reason attribute, swipe and Escape to dismiss, and dark mode.
